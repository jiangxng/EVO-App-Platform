# B9w | Separate topology and presentation fingerprints
Adds an aggregate SHA-256 of sorted business edge identity, kind, source, target to B9k's **local** validated input summary. Existing full graph digest still includes layout/labels; topology digest remains stable over display positions and redacted text but changes when a relation endpoint is reversed. This supports reproducible QA of unchanged business direction during projection edits. **Not an anonymity guarantee:** do not publish hashes of sensitive graph structure without permission. No business mutation or re-versioning, no customer data, §14 39 NOT TESTED.
